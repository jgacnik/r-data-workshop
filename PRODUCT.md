# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Beginner learners who are new to R and are preparing for or following the Data Analysis in R workshop. They need to install and prepare the required tools, work through the lessons, and complete a small analysis.

## Product Purpose

The site supports an introductory Data Analysis in R workshop. Success means learners can prepare their computers, follow the six course parts, and use the starter project to complete a small analysis.

## Positioning

The workshop teaches through practical, hands-on lessons that progress from R basics to a complete project demonstration. This teaching approach was confirmed by the workshop owner.

## Operating Context

Learners use the website before, during, and after the workshop. They follow setup instructions, view interactive presentations in a browser, use RStudio for exercises, and refer to printable PDFs and downloadable project materials. The setup guide covers Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- The website is built with Quarto and published at https://jgacnik.github.io/r-data-workshop/.
- The course has six parts covering R basics, visualization, transformation, tidying, projects and data import, and a complete project demonstration.
- Materials include interactive presentations with webR examples, printable PDFs, and a downloadable starter RStudio project with scripts and data.
- Setup guidance covers R, RStudio Desktop, and required R packages.
- Preserve the existing course scope and documented software workflow. Other product-specific constraints are undecided.
- Retain the existing Quarto source and static HTML/CSS output, hosted on GitHub Pages. The owner confirmed this deployment and technology constraint.

## Brand Commitments

The existing name is “Data Analysis in R.” The site identifies the workshop as developed and led by Asst. Prof. Jan Gačnik and uses the Jožef Stefan International Postgraduate School logo. Preserve these existing identity facts.

The owner requests a smoother, considered redesign that avoids a generic AI-generated appearance. Preserve workshop materials, lesson content, downloads, and the existing software workflow.

Keep lesson numbering, but integrate it into the lesson headings at a readable size rather than using small standalone decorative labels, as explicitly requested by the owner.

## Evidence on Hand

- Six lesson sources: `R_part1.qmd` through `R_part6.qmd`.

- Do not fabricate testimonials, learner outcomes, partnerships, or quantitative impact claims; none are established in the repository or confirmed by the owner.

## Product Principles

- Teach through practical work learners can follow and repeat.
- Carry learners from setup and foundational concepts through a complete analysis workflow.
- Keep the lesson materials, printable references, and starter project aligned with the workshop.
